The Rainbow Bridge at Qingming

清明上河圖・虹橋

The central scene of Zhang Zeduan's handscroll, rebuilt to walk through: the pierless woven-timber arch over the Bian River, its stalls and crowds, the taverns and landings on both banks, and the grain barges that must drop their masts to pass beneath it.

Nothing here is on a loop. Every walker, carter, boatman and hawker keeps their own errand and pace.
